Figure 1. Reporting conventions.
Signed annual relative-GHG intensity in the final CAB impact workbooks. Negative values represent expected reduction/avoidance relative to the source counterfactual, not metered annual performance. Bars show the published summary and two transparent denominator rules applied to the numeric disclosed numerator. The 2023 published summary is reproduced by all allocations; the 2024 summary by numeric-GHG allocations. The all-allocation construction does not assign physical zero to missing projects. 2022 is an extension with an initial-versus-current project-cost-definition change. Units: expected tCO2e/year per EUR million eligible project cost, allocation-weighted.

Figure 2. Sources of reported change.
Contributions to the +121.606034 signed change in the numeric-GHG-allocation ratio. Continuing-project contributions use the symmetric two-factor identity; GHG, cost and eligibility changes use a permutation-average product decomposition. Additions/removals refer to numeric-disclosure-set membership, not construction or cessation. Removal includes three numeric-to-missing transitions (+0.087975) and 65 table exits (+143.501783); all 61 additions are table entrants. Reweighting uses normalized weights in the full numeric disclosure set. These contributions are accounting allocations over linked factors, not causal mechanisms. Units match Figure 1.

Figure 3. Disclosure coverage.
Left: total CAB allocation by semantic GHG disclosure status, including numeric zero and signed positive values among the numeric group. Right: allocation attached to numeric GHG within the source's main eligible-activity macro-sector categories in 2023 and 2024. Main-activity grouping is not necessarily the boundary of whole-project GHG. Deferred, below-threshold and withheld disclosures represent different mechanisms and are not uniformly bounded or imputed. The three 2024 externally reviewed withholding cases cover EUR1,009.832402m. Units: EUR billion and percentage of each main-activity group's allocation.

Figure 4. Sensitivity comparisons.
Percentage change in the magnitude of negative relative-GHG reporting intensity from 2023 to 2024 under explicit population, project-cost and weighting choices. Continuing CAB rows use 48 numeric pairs; combined-boundary continuing rows use 55 pairs after including 2024 EuGBS and counting each whole project once. Whole-cost matched fixed-weight comparisons use normalized 2023 weights. Their small changes contrast with annual reweighting; they do not correct selection or establish physical performance. The full combined-stream row compares 2023 CAB with the 178-project combined 2024 boundary, so it is a financial-boundary sensitivity rather than an asserted fully harmonized historical series. Other fixed reference weights are reported in the data. All ranges and alternatives are deterministic; none is a sampling confidence interval.
