Title: Climate Finance and Reported Carbon Impact: Evidence from EIB Green Bonds

Question and evidence level
How much of annual changes in reported allocation-weighted expected GHG intensity persists when coverage, project membership, cost vintage and financial stream boundaries are made explicit? Primary contrast: 2023 to 2024 CAB, as published in the final impact workbooks. Secondary 2022 comparisons retain the documented initial-authorised to current-authorised cost-vintage change. This is a census of disclosed project-stream-report-year records, not a random sample or realized-emissions/causal evaluation.

Sources and integrity
Read original official 2022–2024 XLSX files without changing them. Check their SHA256 against the source manifest. Re-extract merge-aware rows, retaining source row/cell, literal status, raw subordinate values, report, stream and financial field definitions. Normalize numeric and text eight-digit IDs. A subordinate zero hidden inside a merged status cell is missing, not a disclosed zero. Do not collapse deferred, below-threshold, not-applicable and withheld statuses. Preserve positive relative emissions and genuine numeric zeros. Source licence is not assumed from public availability.

Estimands and primary analysis
Let A be reporting-year CAB allocation (EURm), G signed expected whole-project annual relative GHG (ktCO2e/year), C disclosed whole-project cost (EURm), e disclosed eligible share (O/100). s=1000G/(Ce) reconstructs eligible-cost-normalized reporting intensity; it is not component-level physical abatement. I_obs=sum_observed(A*s)/sum_observed(A). I_all=sum_observed(A*s)/sum_all(A). Coverage c=sum_observed(A)/sum_all(A); I_all=c*I_obs. I_all is a disclosed-numerator ratio, not a total-impact estimate or zero-impact assumption. Preserve negative sign convention; percentage changes in magnitude only when comparing negative endpoints. Absolute GHG provides an auxiliary verification/parallel result.

1. Source reconciliation and descriptive finance coverage, retaining the unresolved 2022 absolute-summary residual without forced correction.
2. Report published, fixed all-allocation and fixed observed-allocation conventions. Symmetric exact coverage decomposition: change(I_all)=mean(c)*change(I_obs)+mean(I_obs)*change(c). Published-summary denominator bridge is an arithmetic reconstruction, not an allegation about preparer intent or errors.
3. Exact observed-portfolio decomposition over numeric-set continuers, additions and removals. Distinguish financial-table entry/exit from within-project GHG disclosure transitions. For continuers use symmetric intensity/reweighting terms. Split intensity change into G, reciprocal C, reciprocal e by permutation-average Shapley identity. This is an accounting allocation over constructed factors; factors are not asserted to be independently manipulable or causal. Check positive finite C/e/A and valid comparable scope before use. Approved EIB share and signed loan are not alternative denominators in the primary decomposition.
4. Repeat on only numeric continuing projects, normalized within each year's matched set; report fixed-old/fixed-new weights. Repeat balanced three-year set with fixed 2022 weights and fixed cost/eligibility vintages. These are selected matched subsets, not representative corrections.
5. Sector allocation and contribution concentration; exact sector-mix/within-sector descriptive decomposition. Retain heterogeneous boundaries, especially manufacturing Scope 3 exclusions. No universal project-effectiveness ranking.
6. Deterministic sensitivity: whole-project cost G/C; 100%-eligible records; renewable-generation activities; exclusions of manufacturing; winsorization declared as a sensitivity only; individual leave-one-project-out, union deletion across years and top-contributor exclusion; no bootstrap, p-values or sampling confidence intervals.
7. Missing-GHG scenario grid and analytical tipping points for stipulated allocation-weighted average missing-record intensity. These are conditional comparisons, neither physically validated bounds nor missing-data estimates. No common threshold imposed on unlike missingness mechanisms.
8. Financial boundary sensitivity: preserve CAB and EuGBS separately, identify seven apparent CAB exits reported in 2024 EuGBS and one dual-stream project. For a combined sensitivity, use combined allocation per project and whole-project cost, retain one whole-project GHG estimate, disclose precision discrepancy and alternative handling. Never sum duplicate whole-project costs or GHG. This is not a fully harmonized historical stream series and cannot identify the regulatory effect.
